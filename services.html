Our services

Grooming, supplies and fresh food — everything your pet needs, handled by people who genuinely love animals.

Grooming Salon

The grooming salon

Our team of experienced groomers is rated Bendigo's best for a reason: gentle handling, careful breed-specific work, and dogs that leave looking — and feeling — fantastic.

  • Full grooms — wash, cut and style for your dog's breed and coat
  • Baths and blow-dries for a fresh, fluffy finish
  • Nail trims and finishing care
  • Puppy first trims — a calm, positive introduction to grooming
Pet Supplies

The supplies shop

A well-stocked shop with quality ranges for dogs, cats and small animals — and staff who'll happily help you choose the right thing for your pet.

  • Quality pet foods for every life stage
  • Toys, beds, leads, collars and everyday essentials
  • Treats, chews and training rewards
  • Friendly, honest advice from people who know pets
Fresh Pet Deli

The fresh pet meat deli

The heart of K9 Deli. Fresh pet meat, bones and natural treats from the deli counter — dinner-time goodness that keeps bowls licked clean.

  • Fresh pet meat, cut for pets of every size
  • Meaty bones for healthy teeth and happy chewers
  • Natural treats and deli favourites
  • Ask us what's fresh today
