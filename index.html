Our story

A Bendigo local for 29 years and counting

K9 Deli has been part of the Bendigo community since 1996 — nearly three decades of baths, trims, treats and tail wags. What started as a neighbourhood pet shop grew into something special: a grooming salon, a supplies store and a fresh pet meat deli, all in one friendly spot on Hopetoun Street.

Our team of groomers is trusted by pet owners right across Bendigo — we're proud to be rated the city's number one grooming salon. Generations of local dogs and cats have passed through our doors, and many of them keep coming back.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to book for grooming?
Yes — our grooming team is popular, so we recommend booking ahead by phone on 03 5442 8880. Walk-ins for the shop and deli are always welcome.
What animals do you groom?
Our salon specialises in dogs of all breeds and sizes — from first puppy trims to full breed clips. Ask our team what's right for your dog's coat.
What does the fresh pet deli sell?
Fresh pet meat, bones and natural treats, prepared at the deli counter. It's the part of the shop that makes tails wag before they even get home.
What are your opening hours?
Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm, and Saturday 9am–1pm. We're closed on Sundays.
Where can I find you?
You'll find us at 14 Hopetoun St, Bendigo VIC 3550 — easy to reach, with grooming, supplies and the deli all under one roof.

Come say hello — tails always welcome.

Drop in for a groom, stock up at the deli, or just bring the dog in for a pat. We'd love to meet you.
